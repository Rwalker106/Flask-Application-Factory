About Cafe & Wifi II

Cafe & Wifi II is a simple directory for finding coffee shops that are good for working, studying, and staying connected.

What this site is for

This project collects useful cafe details in one place so you can quickly compare locations, seating, wifi availability, power outlets, and whether a space is suitable for calls.

What you can browse

Each cafe listing includes the cafe name, image, location, and a set of practical details that help remote workers and students choose the right spot.

Why it exists

Not every coffee shop works well as a place to work. This site makes it easier to find cafes that match your needs before you head out.
